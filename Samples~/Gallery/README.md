# Gallery

Every component in its main states, bound to sample view models: HUD band, boss bar, skill button states (including
windup, invalid, rejected and exposed), operative cards, perks, buttons, form controls, the Power strip with reserve and
queue chips, health bar sizes, stat deltas, panel variants, roster tiers, world-space labels and markers, the menu's holo
surfaces, reveal cards and automation rules.

Open `Gallery.unity` and enter Play mode (a 390 × 844 Game view). The scene's `UIDocument` uses
`Packages/com.veilbreak.gui/Runtime/Settings/VeilbreakPortrait.asset`; assign a 2:3 portrait to `GuiGallery` to fill the
operative art. Hover the HUD resources, perk chips and result cells.

The gallery also demonstrates glitch text, both road signs, merchant offer availability, HP trade consequences, credits and cyan/violet rarity cards.
