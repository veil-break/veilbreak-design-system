# Ownership and redistribution

Project-authored Veilbreak code, styles, icons, the mark and design tokens retain their original ownership. No new public redistribution license is granted by this extraction. Publish to private repositories unless the rights holder chooses a public license. Unity package dependencies retain their own licenses.

The bundled fonts keep their own free licenses, which permit embedding and redistribution in games and apps; see `Third Party Notices.md` and `Runtime/Fonts/FONTS-LICENSE.md`.
